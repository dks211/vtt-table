# East Tennessee 1861 implementation refinements

These requirements refine existing vertical slices. They do not create additional slices and must not be treated as authorization to implement unfinished scenes.

## Lick Creek reconnaissance

Reconnaissance must report the detail actually present rather than assuming the ordinary six-man detail. From a suitable observation point, characters can count plainly visible guards without a roll and recognize an alert posture: weapons kept close, limited sleep, watched approaches, and irregular or overlapping patrols.

Roll only for risky concealment, unusually close observation, or information that is not plainly visible. Map measurements and sight-line tools remain advisory GM aids, never independent legality checks.

## Lick Creek withdrawal ending

A clean withdrawal from a visibly reinforced and alert detail is a legitimate ending. If the party judges an attack suicidal and withdraws without exposure, they preserve their lives and operational secrecy, Lick Creek remains intact, distant bridge fires appear from the ridge, and their assigned bridge remains dark. The ending must acknowledge both prudent judgment and the cost of the incomplete mission; it must not characterize withdrawal as cowardice or player failure.

## Finch's Nest geography

The Finch's Nest slice requires functional ground- and upper-floor geography sufficient for the dispatch-case theft. It must define the common room, bar, kitchen/service area, entrances, stairs, upstairs corridor, Will's room, adjacent occupied rooms, staff sleeping location, windows, stable and yard, the stable roof's relationship to upstairs access, and ordinary nighttime light, noise, and movement.

Architectural simulation is unnecessary, but a clean theft-and-return plan must operate against concrete geography.

## Structured-round transition

Reconnaissance, waiting, travel, and ordinary patrol observation use unstructured minutes. Structured rounds begin when immediate opposing action starts or when a countdown or active process must advance relative to character actions. A stealth attempt alone does not automatically start structured rounds.
